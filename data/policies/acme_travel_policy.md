# ACME TECHNOLOGIES

# CORPORATE TRAVEL AND EXPENSE POLICY

**Policy ID:** TEP-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 01-Nov-2026  
**Document Type:** Demonstration Policy

> **NOTE:** All limits below are fictional demonstration values created exclusively for this capstone project.

## 1. Employee Categories

**Executive:**  
Senior leadership employees.

**Standard:**  
Regular full-time employees.

**Contractor:**  
Approved contractual employees.

## 2. Flight Booking

**Executive:**  
Business class permitted.

**Standard:**  
Economy class required.

**Contractor:**  
Economy class required.

International business-class exceptions require written executive authorization.

## 3. Advance Booking

Flights should normally be booked at least 14 calendar days before departure.

Bookings within 14 days require business justification and manager review.

## 4. Hotel Accommodation

**Executive:**  
Maximum INR 12,000 per night.

**Standard:**  
Maximum INR 6,000 per night.

**Contractor:**  
Maximum INR 4,000 per night.

Exceptions require manager approval.

## 5. Meal Allowance

**Executive:**  
Maximum INR 2,500 per day.

**Standard:**  
Maximum INR 1,500 per day.

**Contractor:**  
Maximum INR 1,000 per day.

Amounts exceeding the applicable limit require justification and finance review.

## 6. Receipt Requirements

Receipts are mandatory for individual expenses exceeding INR 1,000.

Missing receipts require finance review.

## 7. Mileage Reimbursement

Eligible personal vehicle travel is reimbursed at INR 12 per kilometre.

A valid business purpose and travel distance must be recorded.

## 8. Approval Hierarchy

**Expenses up to INR 10,000:**  
Line Manager.

**Expenses above INR 10,000:**  
Line Manager followed by Finance.

**Expenses above INR 50,000:**  
Line Manager, Department Head and Finance.

## 9. Policy Exceptions

All policy exceptions require documented justification.

Hard restrictions cannot be overridden by the AI agent.

Final approval authority remains with designated human approvers.
